## What I do

I lead **Team Devdoots**, a six-person engineering team building products for Indian communities — and I write the architecture, the backend, and a good share of the AI layer myself.

My work sits in one narrow but useful gap: **taking a technology nobody has pointed at a real problem yet, and pointing it there.** Satellite imagery for rooftop leasing. Pose estimation for distress detection. Whisper for turning a two-hour lecture into ten postable reels. In each case the interesting part was never the model — it was the loop around it that makes it usable by a person who has never heard of the model.

Three things I care about in every repo I ship:

- **It runs on a laptop.** No GPU, no paid tier, no "coming soon." If a student in Lucknow cannot run it, it is not finished.
- **The README tells the truth.** What works, what does not, what was measured, what was mocked. Every project below has a *Known limitations* section, and it is not decorative.
- **The team can explain every line.** I review PRs, not just merge them.

### 🛰️ VyomAcre
**Rooftop leasing, verified from orbit.**

India has idle rooftops. Businesses need land for solar, telecom and logistics. Today a broker and a two-week site visit sit in between. VyomAcre removes both: an owner lists a roof in two minutes, **Google Earth Engine** measures it from satellite imagery, an admin signs off, and lease requests arrive in the owner's inbox.

The full loop runs in production — signup → list → verify → request → accept → contact unlock.

### 👁️ NETRA
**See. Detect. Protect.**

A CCTV camera records. It does not understand. NETRA turns a passive feed into an active emergency-response engine: it counts people, recognises a **sustained SOS gesture — wrist above the shoulder, held three seconds** — and on confirmation writes a forensic incident report with Gemini Vision and archives the evidence to Google Drive while the dashboard raises the alarm.

Runs on a webcam, a phone IP camera or an RTSP stream. No GPU.

### 🎬 Auto Reels Studio
**Long video in, postable reels out. Free.**

Transcribes a lecture, podcast or webinar with open-source Whisper, finds the topic boundaries, reframes each segment to 9:16, burns in styled captions **including Hindi**, and packages a ready-to-post ZIP. Pulls source video from a file, a YouTube/Instagram/X/Vimeo link, or Drive.

No watermark, no clip limit, no subscription.

## How I run a team

Six people, one protected `main`, and rules that are written down rather than assumed. Every task is scoped to a **45–60 minute focus block**. Every PR gets a review against a checklist before it merges — does it run, are the names right, is the proof-of-work log filled in. Every evening there is a five-minute technical standup where each member explains their own work out loud, because a commit nobody can explain is a commit you cannot debug.

The result is a team where the repos read like a company's, not a classroom's.
